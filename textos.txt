Aero Performance & Aero Design are not the same.
The Aero Performance Engineer is the one in charge of Analysing track data, understanding performance losses after crashes or wind gusts and ensuring the car performs as expected.
Coding proficiency is required to pull, manipulate and interpret the data.
The Aero Designer, on the other hand, Designs the aerodynamic package of the car, requiring a deeper understanding of how air particles travel around the car.
